Financial Support

Foreign Scholarships

International scholarships are given on the basis of qualifications, needs, occupations, career and universities. Talk with our counsellors to know more about scholarships for both undergraduate and postgraduate studies abroad.

01

Merits

Relief is offered on a merit-based basis — a scheme that began to evolve primarily as top-tier colleges competed for the brightest and most eligible candidates. Prizes are awarded for outstanding students based on their accomplishments in academics, athletics and even extra-curricular programmes.

02

Profession

Universities offer financial assistance in developing talent to tackle supply shortages. Students who seek jobs in expertise-shortage domains obtain significant financial assistance — intensely competitive, usually in the form of reduced tuition fees.

03

Needs

Financial support is one of the main requirements for which students are eligible. Financially less-fortunate students earn supports by taking their academic performance into account. Register with GGE and get financial aid for tuition and living costs, entirely or partly.

04

Student Characteristics

When awarding scholarships, distinctive student characteristics are considered — medical conditions, age, gender or other differentiating factors. Also known as minority-based scholarships, these benefits target groups that deserve equal opportunities.

Student Life in the UK

The United Kingdom is famous for great monuments and historical importance. Half of the high-ranked universities are in the UK — a source of attraction for millions of students. Every year, more than 400,000 students come to the UK for education purposes. Scholarships, structure, advantages and high-quality education are the main reasons for the prominent increment in students applying to the UK.

Manners & Behaviours

Adaptation of multiple cultures can be seen throughout the UK — colonies, streets and markets influenced by particular cultures. The UK absorbs multiple traditions and customs, blending them well. Everyone has respect for each other and greets with a smile.

Food

As the UK is an amalgam of various well-known countries, there's a wide range of cuisines — Asian, Mexican, Continental, Seafood, Indian and more. Restaurants, cafés and food streets offer vegan and non-vegan options with costs varying from place to place.
